# Overview

guavacheck is an AI-powered property intelligence platform designed to transform how people design, build, buy, sell, verify, manage, and invest in real estate.

Rather than functioning as a traditional property listing website, guavacheck serves as a complete property ecosystem where artificial intelligence assists users throughout the entire property lifecycle—from the first architectural drawing to ownership, valuation, maintenance, investment, and eventual resale.

At the heart of the platform is **Austin**, an intelligent AI engine capable of coordinating multiple specialist systems to provide accurate, explainable recommendations for property-related decisions.
