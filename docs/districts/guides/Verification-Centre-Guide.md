\# Verification Centre Guide



\## Overview



The Verification Centre is the authentication and validation authority of Guava City.



Its responsibility is to ensure that every important entity entering the ecosystem can be trusted.



Verification is not a one-time activity.



It is a continuous intelligence process.



\---



\# Mission



The Verification Centre exists to reduce fraud, improve transparency, and increase confidence across every transaction.



Every verified entity becomes more valuable.
