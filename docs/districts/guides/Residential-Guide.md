\# Residential District Guide



\## Overview



The Residential District helps users discover homes that match their lifestyle, family needs, and future aspirations.



Unlike traditional property portals, the Residential District begins with \*how you want to live\* rather than \*what you want to buy\*.



\---

\- Families

\- Relocating professionals

\- Diaspora buyers
