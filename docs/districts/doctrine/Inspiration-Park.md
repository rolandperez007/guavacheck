\# Inspiration Park Doctrine\## PurposeInspiration Park exists to stimulate creativity, education and innovation.Ideas become buildings here.\---\## Core PhilosophyKnowledge inspires creation.Creation inspires progress.\---\## Major Areas\- Learning Center\- Design Gallery\- Architecture Library\- Innovation Studio\- Guava Architect Program\---\## Guava Architect ProgramVerified architects may:\- publish designs\- sell concepts\- create templates\- collaborate with buildersDesigns become reusable digital assets.\---\## Austin ResponsibilitiesAustin becomes:Creative Design Mentor.Austin assists with:\- architectural inspiration\- education\- design recommendations\- creative exploration\---\## Long-term GoalBecome the world's largest architectural inspiration platform.
