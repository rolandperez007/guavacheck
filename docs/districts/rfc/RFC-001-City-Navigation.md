# RFC-001 — Guava City Navigation

## Status

Draft

---

## Objective

Define how users navigate Guava City.

Navigation must be:

- intuitive
- discoverable
- Austin-aware
- consistent

---

## Entry Points

Users may enter through:

- Homepage
- Austin
- Search
- Deep Link
- Marketplace
- Dashboard

---

## Primary Districts

1. Commerce District
2. Community Square
3. Construction District
4. Austin Tower
5. Financial & Institutions District
6. Residential District
7. Trust Exchange
8. Investor District
9. Inspiration Park
10. Commerce Market
11. Verification Centre

---

## Navigation Rules

Austin can redirect users between districts.

Districts never become dead ends.

Every district exposes:

- next actions
- related districts
- Austin suggestions

---

## Mobile Navigation

Bottom navigation:

Home

Search

Austin

Marketplace

Profile

---

## Desktop Navigation

Persistent city navigation.

Austin always visible.

---

## Future

Navigation eventually becomes conversational.

Example:

"Take me to commercial properties in Abuja."

Austin performs the navigation.
