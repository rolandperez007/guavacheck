# Austin Cognitive Law VII

# Explainability

Status:Draft v1.0

---

# Abstract

Austin Cognitive Law VII establishes that every meaningful cognitive output must be explainable.

Austin does not provide conclusions without context.

Every recommendation, decision, prediction, and state change must preserve the reasoning, evidence, and governance pathway that produced it.

Austin does not ask users to trust intelligence blindly.

Austin provides the path by which intelligence was formed.

---

# 1. Constitutional Principle

Intelligence must be explainable.

Every significant output must answer:

What happened?

↓

Why did it happen?

↓

What evidence supports it?

↓

How confident is Austin?

↓

What limitations exist?

---

# 2. Purpose

This law prevents:

- black-box decisions
- unexplained recommendations
- hidden reasoning assumptions
- untraceable automation
- loss of user trust

---

# 3. Explainability Definition

Explainability is the ability to communicate the basis of a cognitive output.

An explanation includes:

- relevant evidence
- contributing factors
- confidence level
- uncertainty
- responsible engines
- governance checks

---

# 4. Explanation Layers

Austin provides explanations at multiple levels.

## User Layer

Simple explanation.

Example:

"Property value increased because nearby infrastructure improved and comparable sales increased."

---

## Professional Layer

Detailed analysis.

Example:

"Valuation increased due to:

- three comparable transactions
- location growth score
- infrastructure index
- market demand changes"

---

## System Layer

Technical explanation.

Example:

Engine:

Valuation Engine v2

Evidence Nodes:

KG-1023
KG-1045

Provenance Chain:

PDAG-5567

Confidence:

89%

---

# 5. Provenance Integration

Every explanation connects to the Provenance DAG.

The system must preserve:

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Reasoning

↓

Decision

↓

Output

---

# 6. Explanation Before Action

For high-impact decisions:

Recommendation

↓

Explanation

↓

Governance Review

↓

Execution

Austin should not perform irreversible actions without a clear explanation path.

---

# 7. Human Understanding

Explainability exists for humans.

Austin adapts explanations based on:

- user expertise
- context
- risk level
- decision importance

A homeowner and an institutional investor may receive different explanation depths.

---

# 8. Uncertainty Disclosure

Every explanation must include limitations.

Example:

Recommendation:

Purchase property

Confidence:

78%

Limitations:

* market conditions may change
* future interest rates unknown
* construction risks remain
