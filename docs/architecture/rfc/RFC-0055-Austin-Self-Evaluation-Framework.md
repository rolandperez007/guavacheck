# RFC-0055

# Austin Self-Evaluation Framework

**Status:** Draft v1.0  
**Category:** Cognitive Intelligence Architecture  
**System:** Austin Cognitive Operating System  
**Maintainer:** Guava Networks Limited

---

# Abstract

The Austin Self-Evaluation Framework (ASEF) enables Austin to continuously assess the quality, reliability, completeness, consistency, and constitutional compliance of its own cognitive processes.

Austin does not assume that its reasoning is correct.

Austin measures its own performance before trusting its conclusions.

Self-evaluation is therefore an essential component of trustworthy intelligence rather than an optional diagnostic feature.

---

# 1. Purpose

The Self-Evaluation Framework provides:

- reasoning quality assessment
- confidence validation
- constitutional compliance verification
- contradiction detection
- completeness analysis
- recommendation scoring
- continuous cognitive improvement

---

# 2. Core Principle

Every important cognitive result should be evaluated before it is committed or presented.

```
Reasoning

↓

Evaluation

↓

Decision

↓

Execution
``` id="evaluation-core"

Austin evaluates itself before asking humans to trust it.

---

# 3. Evaluation Targets

Austin evaluates:

- observations
- reasoning graphs
- simulations
- predictions
- recommendations
- generated content
- execution plans

Evaluation applies across the entire cognitive pipeline.

---

# 4. Evaluation Criteria

Every cognitive artifact is scored against:

- correctness
- consistency
- completeness
- confidence
- provenance quality
- constitutional compliance
- explainability

Each criterion produces an independent score.

---

# 5. Confidence Calibration

Austin compares:

```
Predicted Confidence

↓

Observed Outcome
``` id="confidence"

Repeated differences trigger recalibration of future confidence estimates.

---

# 6. Contradiction Detection

Self-evaluation identifies internal inconsistencies.

Example:

```
Reasoning A

↓

Conclusion X

Reasoning B

↓

Conclusion Y
``` id="contradiction"

Austin pauses execution until reconciliation or governance review.

---

# 7. Completeness Analysis

Austin determines whether sufficient evidence exists.

Possible outcomes:

- complete
- partially complete
- insufficient evidence

Insufficient evidence results in uncertainty rather than unsupported conclusions.

---

# 8. Constitutional Compliance

Every evaluation verifies adherence to Austin's Constitutional Laws.

Checks include:

- Reality Is Authoritative
- Observation Precedes Knowledge
- Knowledge Requires Provenance
- Explainability
- Human Authority Supremacy

Constitutional violations block execution.

---

# 9. Recommendation Quality

Recommendations receive an overall quality score.

Factors include:

- evidence strength
- reasoning depth
- prediction reliability
- simulation coverage
- institutional consistency

Austin may refuse to recommend if quality falls below governance thresholds.

---

# 10. Learning Feedback

Evaluation results feed directly into the Learning Engine.

```
Execution

↓

Outcome

↓

Evaluation

↓

Learning
``` id="feedback"

Austin improves through measured performance rather than assumptions.

---

# 11. Human Review

High-impact decisions may require mandatory human evaluation.

Examples:

- large investments
- legal transfers
- institutional approvals
- regulatory submissions

Austin identifies situations where human oversight remains appropriate.

---

# 12. GuavaCheck Example

Property valuation.

Austin evaluates:

- evidence quality
- comparable properties
- market model reliability
- prediction confidence
- constitutional compliance

Only then is the valuation released.

# 14. Architectural Importance

Traditional AI often produces answers without evaluating its own reasoning.

Austin evaluates its cognition before trusting it.

This creates:

- higher reliability
- improved transparency
- measurable quality
- continuous improvement

Austin becomes capable of questioning itself.

---

# 15. Summary

The Austin Self-Evaluation Framework enables Austin to critically assess its own intelligence.

Every conclusion is measured.

Every recommendation is scored.

Every important cognitive decision is validated before execution.

Trust becomes the result of continuous self-assessment rather than blind confidence.
