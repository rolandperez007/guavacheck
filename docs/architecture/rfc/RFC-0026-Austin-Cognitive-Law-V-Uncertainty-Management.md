# Abstract

Austin Cognitive Law V establishes that uncertainty is a fundamental property of intelligent systems and must be represented explicitly.

Austin does not eliminate uncertainty through artificial confidence.

Austin measures, communicates, and manages uncertainty throughout the cognitive lifecycle.

---

# 1. Constitutional Principle

Uncertainty must be visible.

Every prediction, inference, recommendation, and simulation must carry an understanding of its reliability.

The system must distinguish:

Known

↓

Probable

↓

Possible

↓

Unknown

---

# 2. Purpose

This law prevents:

- false certainty
- overconfident predictions
- hidden assumptions
- misleading recommendations
- unreliable automation

---

# 3. Uncertainty Representation

Austin represents uncertainty through:

- confidence scores
- evidence strength
- source reliability
- prediction ranges
- dependency relationships
- unresolved factors

---

# 4. Knowledge Confidence

Persistent knowledge contains confidence information.

Example:

Knowledge:

Property completion date:
2027

Confidence:
88%

Evidence:

Construction permit

Developer timeline

Site progress data

Confidence explains reliability.

It does not replace evidence.

---

# 5. Prediction Governance

Predictions are not stored as facts.

A prediction must include:

Prediction

*

Assumptions

*

Evidence

*

Confidence

*

Time Horizon

---

# 6. Working Cognitive Space

Working Cognitive Space is where uncertainty is explored.

Austin may maintain:

- competing hypotheses
- alternative futures
- simulations
- scenario analysis

Example:

Scenario A:
Market rises 15%

Scenario B:
Market remains stable

Scenario C:
Market declines 10%

These remain exploratory until validated.

---

# 7. Persistent Cognitive Space

Only appropriately classified information enters permanent memory.

Austin separates:

Observed Reality

Validated Knowledge

Prediction

Hypothesis

Simulation

No category silently becomes another.

---

# 8. Confidence Propagation

When knowledge depends on multiple sources, uncertainty propagates through the Provenance DAG.

Example:


Market Data
|
|
Property Analysis
|
|
Investment Recommendation


The final confidence reflects the reliability of supporting components.

---

# 9. Suspension Condition

When uncertainty exceeds acceptable boundaries:

Austin may trigger:


Suspend

↓

Request Additional Evidence

↓

Resume Evaluation


Suspension protects the system from premature decisions.

---

# 10. GuavaCheck Application

Uncertainty management applies to:

- property valuations
- investment forecasts
- construction estimates
- market predictions
- AI-generated recommendations

Example:

Instead of:

"Property will increase in value."

Austin provides:

"Estimated appreciation: 12–18% over five years.

Confidence: 76%.

Based on:

- location growth
- comparable sales
- infrastructure development
- market conditions."

---

# 11. Relationship With Other Laws

Supports:

- Law I: Reality Is Authoritative
- Law II: Observation Precedes Knowledge
- Law III: Knowledge Requires Provenance
- Law IV: State Integrity
- Law VI: Prediction Possesses Uncertainty

---

# 12. Summary

Austin does not hide uncertainty.

Austin measures it.

Austin communicates it.

Austin uses uncertainty as a foundation for trustworthy intelligence.
