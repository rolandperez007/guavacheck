# RFC-0042

# Austin Knowledge Evolution

**Status:** Draft v1.0  
**Category:** Knowledge Architecture  
**System:** Austin Cognitive Operating System  
**Maintainer:** Guava Networks Limited

---

# Abstract

Austin Knowledge Evolution defines how knowledge is created, refined, corrected, expanded, deprecated, and preserved throughout the lifetime of the Austin Cognitive Operating System.

Knowledge is not static.

Knowledge is a living structure that evolves as reality evolves.

Austin never "updates" knowledge.

Austin evolves knowledge while preserving history.

---

# 1. Purpose

Knowledge Evolution provides:

- continuous learning
- historical preservation
- contradiction resolution
- evidence accumulation
- confidence refinement
- knowledge versioning
- institutional continuity

---

# 2. Core Principle

Knowledge never changes silently.

Every change becomes a new stage in the evolution of truth.

```
Observation

↓

Validation

↓

Knowledge Evolution

↓

Persistent Knowledge
``` id="knowledge-core"

---

# 3. Evolution Pipeline

Austin evolves knowledge through a deterministic process.

```
Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Validation

↓

Governance

↓

Evolution

↓

Commit
``` id="evolution-pipeline"

Skipping stages is prohibited.

``` id="knowledge-version"

Previous versions remain permanently available.

# 5. Confidence Evolution

Confidence changes through evidence.

Example:

```
Initial Confidence

0.42

↓

Survey

↓

0.76

↓

Government Registry

↓

0.98
``` id="confidence"

It is never overwritten.

---

# 6. Contradictions

Contradictory observations trigger evolution rather than replacement.

Example:

```
Registry

↓

Owner A

Survey

↓

Owner B
``` id="contradiction"

Austin suspends certainty until governance resolves the conflict.

---

# 7. Deprecation

Knowledge may become obsolete.

Deprecated knowledge:

- remains accessible
- retains provenance
- is excluded from active reasoning

Austin forgets nothing.

---

# 8. Evidence Accumulation

Knowledge strengthens through multiple observations.

```
Observation

↓

Evidence Stack

↓

Knowledge
``` id="evidence"

Austin reasons over accumulated evidence rather than isolated facts.

---

# 9. Provenance Preservation

Every evolution retains:

- observation lineage
- evidence
- governance decisions
- reasoning chain
- timestamps

Knowledge without provenance cannot evolve.

---

# 10. Human Corrections

Human-authorized corrections create new knowledge versions.

Example:

```
Human Review

↓

Correction

↓

Knowledge v5
``` id="human-correction"

History remains intact.

---

# 11. Synthetic Knowledge

AI-generated hypotheses remain separate.

Classification:

```
Observed

Validated

Synthetic

Predicted
``` id="classification"

Synthetic information never becomes authoritative knowledge without evidence.

---

# 12. Digital Twins

Digital Twins continuously evolve.

Changes include:

- renovations
- maintenance
- valuations
- inspections
- ownership
- environmental conditions

Knowledge evolution updates the twin rather than replacing it.

# 15. Architectural Importance

Traditional databases overwrite records.

Austin evolves knowledge.

This distinction enables:

- explainability
- historical reasoning
- reproducibility
- institutional memory
- trustworthy AI

Austin becomes progressively wiser rather than merely larger.

# 16. Summary

Knowledge is not a snapshot.

Knowledge is a continuously evolving understanding of reality.

Austin preserves every stage of that evolution, ensuring that truth becomes richer over time without losing its history.
