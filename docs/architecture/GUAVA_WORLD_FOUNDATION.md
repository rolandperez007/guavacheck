# GUAVA WORLD FOUNDATION

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational Architecture
**Project:** Guava Engineering Intelligence Platform

---

# Vision

Guava is an Engineering Intelligence Platform designed to understand, design, estimate, validate, construct, operate, monitor, maintain and continuously improve physical infrastructure anywhere in the world.

Guava is not simply a CAD application.

Guava is not simply a BIM application.

Guava is not simply an estimating platform.

Guava is not simply an AI chatbot.

Guava is an intelligent engineering ecosystem where artificial intelligence, engineering science, construction knowledge, regional intelligence and digital twins work together as one continuously evolving system.

---

# Mission

To make world-class engineering knowledge accessible to everyone while respecting local standards, environmental conditions, construction methods, regulations and economic realities.

Every user—from a homeowner building a single room to governments planning smart cities—should receive engineering recommendations comparable to those produced by experienced multidisciplinary engineering teams.

---

# Core Principles

Every decision inside Guava must follow these principles.

## 1. Truth Before Convenience

Engineering decisions must always be based on verified engineering knowledge rather than assumptions.

---

## 2. Regional Intelligence

No engineering recommendation is universal.

Every recommendation must consider:

* Country
* State
* City
* Climate
* Soil
* Geology
* Local regulations
* Material availability
* Labor practices
* Utility infrastructure
* Environmental conditions

---

## 3. Single Source of Truth

Knowledge shall exist only once.

No duplicated engineering logic.

No duplicated engineering calculations.

No duplicated standards.

No duplicated material definitions.

Every subsystem consumes the same knowledge foundation.

---

## 4. Explainability

Every recommendation generated by Guava must be explainable.

Every calculation must have traceable inputs.

Every recommendation must reference the engineering knowledge used to generate it.

Users must always understand why Guava recommends something.

---

## 5. Continuous Learning

Guava continuously evolves.

New standards.

New materials.

New technologies.

New manufacturers.

New construction methods.

New regional data.

Knowledge expands.

Architecture remains stable.
