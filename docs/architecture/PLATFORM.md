# Platform Overview

GuavaCheck is an AI-powered Property Intelligence Platform.

The platform is designed as a modular, event-driven enterprise system.

## Core Principles

- Domain Driven Design
- Modular Architecture
- Event-Driven Communication
- Service-Oriented Modules
- Workflow-Oriented Business Processes
- Multi-Tenant Institutions
- AI-Assisted Decision Support

## Primary Platforms

- Institution Platform
- Workflow Platform
- Property Passport Platform
- Billing Platform
- Simulation Platform
- Notification Platform
- Austin AI Platform
- Community Platform
- Analytics Platform

Each platform owns its own models, services, repositories, APIs, analytics, events, and permissions.

Cross-platform communication occurs through well-defined service interfaces and domain events rather than direct coupling.
