# Vision

The Austin Facility Management Engine enables intelligent operation, monitoring, optimization, and management of buildings throughout their entire operational life.

Construction creates buildings.

Operations create value.

Austin ensures that every completed asset continues to perform efficiently, safely, and intelligently.

---

# Mission

Enable Austin to:

- Monitor building performance
- Optimize operations
- Reduce operating costs
- Improve occupant experience
- Predict operational issues
- Automate facility workflows

---

# Core Principle

A building is not finished when construction ends.

A building becomes an intelligent asset through continuous operation, learning, and improvement.
