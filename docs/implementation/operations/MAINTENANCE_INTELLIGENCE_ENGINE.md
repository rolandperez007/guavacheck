# Vision

The Austin Maintenance Intelligence Engine enables buildings, infrastructure, and equipment to become self-aware, predictive, and continuously optimized.

Austin moves maintenance from reactive repair to intelligent prevention.

Instead of waiting for failures, Austin predicts problems before they occur.

- Reduce downtime

- Improve asset reliability
- Reduce operational costs
- Extend asset lifespan
