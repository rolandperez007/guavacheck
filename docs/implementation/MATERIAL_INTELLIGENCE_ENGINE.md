Version: 1.0

Status: Core Engineering Intelligence

Owner: Austin Engineering Division

# Vision

The Material Intelligence Engine enables Austin to understand construction materials, evaluate their suitability, predict long-term performance, estimate costs, assess environmental impact, and recommend optimal combinations for every project.

Austin understands materials the way an experienced engineer understands them—not merely as products, but as systems with structural, economic, environmental, and lifecycle implications.

Provide intelligent material recommendations from concept design through demolition.
