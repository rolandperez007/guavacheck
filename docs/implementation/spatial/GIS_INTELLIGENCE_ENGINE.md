# Vision

The Austin GIS Intelligence Engine provides the spatial foundation for every property, building, infrastructure asset, and geographic entity managed by Austin.

It transforms maps into intelligent knowledge by integrating geospatial data, engineering models, Digital Twins, AI reasoning, and Property Passports into a unified spatial intelligence platform.

Austin understands not only where assets are located, but also how they relate to their environment, infrastructure, regulations, risks, and future development opportunities.
