# Vision

The Austin Satellite and Remote Sensing Engine continuously observes the Earth's surface using satellite imagery, aerial photography, drone surveys, LiDAR, radar, hyperspectral sensors, and emerging Earth observation technologies.

Austin transforms raw imagery into actionable property, infrastructure, environmental, and urban intelligence that powers Digital Twins, Property Passports, predictive simulations, and AI reasoning.

Enable Austin to:

- Monitor land globally
- Detect environmental change
- Observe construction activity
