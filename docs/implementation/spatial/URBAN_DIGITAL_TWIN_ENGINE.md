# Vision

The Austin Urban Digital Twin Engine creates living digital representations of neighborhoods, campuses, industrial parks, cities, metropolitan regions, and national infrastructure.

Rather than modeling individual buildings in isolation, Austin continuously synchronizes physical assets, infrastructure, environmental systems, utilities, mobility networks, construction activities, and operational intelligence into a unified city-scale Digital Twin.

The Urban Digital Twin becomes the operational intelligence layer for governments, developers, engineers, investors, facility operators, emergency services, and citizens.

Enable Austin to:
