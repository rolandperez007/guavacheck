# Vision

The Austin Workflow Orchestration Engine coordinates every long-running business process across the Austin ecosystem. It provides intelligent execution, monitoring, recovery, and optimization of workflows spanning engineering, construction, finance, legal, operations, government, and marketplace services.

Rather than executing isolated tasks, Austin manages complete business processes from initiation to completion while continuously adapting to changing conditions.

---

# Mission

Enable Austin to:

- Orchestrate enterprise workflows
- Coordinate distributed services
- Manage long-running processes
- Automate approvals
- Synchronize intelligence engines
- Recover from failures
- Optimize execution paths
- Support human collaboration
- Maintain process transparency
- Continuously improve operations
