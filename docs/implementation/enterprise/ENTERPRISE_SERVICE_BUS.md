# Vision

The Austin Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) provides the communication backbone that connects every intelligence engine, business service, external institution, AI agent, Digital Twin, Property Passport, and marketplace participant into a unified event-driven ecosystem.

Rather than point-to-point integrations, Austin adopts a loosely coupled architecture where services communicate through standardized events, commands, queries, and workflows.

The Enterprise Service Bus enables Austin to scale from a single application into a globally distributed Property Intelligence Operating System.
