# Vision

The Austin Legal Intelligence Engine creates an intelligent legal framework that helps property owners, developers, institutions, legal professionals, and governments understand, verify, and manage legal information throughout the entire property lifecycle.

Austin transforms fragmented legal documents into structured, searchable, explainable legal intelligence.

---

# Mission

Enable Austin to:

- Verify legal ownership
- Analyze legal documents
- Detect legal risks
- Improve transaction transparency
- Assist legal professionals
- Build institutional trust

---

# Core Principle

Every property has a legal history.

Austin ensures that legal history becomes understandable, verifiable, and permanently connected to the Property Passport.
