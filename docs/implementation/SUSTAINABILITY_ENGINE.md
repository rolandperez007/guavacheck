# Vision

The Sustainability Engine enables Austin to design, evaluate and continuously optimize environmentally responsible developments across their entire lifecycle.

Austin considers sustainability from the first concept sketch through demolition and material recycling.

Sustainability is not a feature.

It is an engineering constraint considered during every decision.

---

# Mission

Help governments, developers, institutions and homeowners create buildings that are safer, healthier, more efficient and environmentally responsible.

# Guiding Principle

The most sustainable building is not necessarily the one with the newest technology.

It is the building that intelligently balances performance, cost, durability, occupant wellbeing and environmental responsibility over its entire lifecycle.

Austin continuously seeks that balance through explainable AI.
