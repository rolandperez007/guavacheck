# Vision

The Austin AI Engineer System provides engineering intelligence capable of analyzing, optimizing, and supporting the complete engineering lifecycle of buildings and infrastructure.

Austin combines engineering knowledge, simulation capability, digital twins, and accumulated project intelligence to assist engineers in creating safer, more efficient, and more sustainable structures.

Austin does not replace professional engineers.

Austin enhances engineering capability through continuous analysis, simulation, and intelligent assistance.

---

# Mission

Enable Austin to:

- Analyze engineering problems
- Support design decisions
- Evaluate structural systems
- Coordinate engineering disciplines
- Identify risks
- Optimize performance

- Learn from engineering outcomes
