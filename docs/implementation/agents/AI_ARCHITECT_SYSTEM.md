# Vision

The Austin AI Architect System provides architectural intelligence capable of understanding human requirements, generating design concepts, evaluating spatial solutions, and collaborating with professional architects throughout the design lifecycle.

Austin does not replace architects.

Austin expands architectural capability by providing computational creativity, analysis, simulation, and continuous optimization.

Enable Austin to:
