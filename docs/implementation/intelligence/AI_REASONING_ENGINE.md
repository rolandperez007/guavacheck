It transforms Austin from an information system into an intelligent decision partner.

Austin does not simply answer questions.

# Explainable AI

Austin must explain decisions.

Every recommendation includes:

## Reason

Why this recommendation exists.

## Evidence

What information supports it.

## Confidence

How certain Austin is.

## Alternatives

Other possible solutions.

---

Example:

Recommendation:

"Replace cooling system."

Reason:

"Energy consumption increased 35% over six months."

Evidence:

"Sensor data indicates reduced efficiency."

Confidence:

87%.

Alternative:

"Repair existing equipment."
