# Digital Twin Definition

A Digital Twin is a dynamic digital representation of a physical asset containing:

- Geometry
- Materials
- Systems
- Sensors
- Documentation
- History
- Current condition
- Operational data
- Future predictions

The Digital Twin becomes the single source of truth throughout the asset lifecycle.
