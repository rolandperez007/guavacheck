# The Charter of Guava Inc.

Version: 1.0

Status: Constitutional Document

Owner: Guava Inc.

Classification: Permanent

---

# Preamble

Guava Inc. was founded on a simple belief:

Technology should make people more confident.

Not more confused.

Not more dependent.

Not more vulnerable.

Every product we build should reduce uncertainty and increase opportunity.

This Charter defines who we are, how we make decisions, and the standards we refuse to compromise.

Technologies will evolve.

Markets will change.

Leadership will change.

This Charter should endure.

---

# Our Purpose

Guava Inc. exists to solve meaningful problems through trustworthy technology.

We build systems that help people make better decisions about property, infrastructure, communities, investment and the built environment.

Our success is measured not only by growth, but by the confidence our users place in us.

---

# Our Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted technology companies for property, infrastructure and intelligent decision-making.

We envision a future where anyone, anywhere, can make complex property decisions with confidence because Guava provides clarity.

---

# Our Mission

To create intelligent platforms that simplify the world's most important property and infrastructure decisions.

Every product should save time.

Reduce risk.

Increase transparency.

Create opportunity.

Strengthen trust.

Products evolve.

---

# Decision Framework

When facing difficult decisions, ask:

Does this increase trust?

Does this improve the user's life?

Does this align with our mission?

Can we explain this decision openly?

Will we still be proud of this decision ten years from now?

If the answer is uncertain, we pause and reconsider.

---

# What We Will Never Become

We will never knowingly deceive users.

We will never prioritize growth above integrity.

We will never sacrifice security for convenience.

We will never misuse user data.

We will never build technology that undermines trust for short-term gain.

We would rather grow more slowly than compromise our principles.

# Closing Declaration

We believe technology should empower people.

We believe intelligence should be guided by integrity.

We believe trust is earned through consistent action.

We believe the future is built one principled decision at a time.

We are building trust.

And that responsibility is greater than any technology we will ever create.
