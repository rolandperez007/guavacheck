\# Government Integration Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for integrating government institutions into the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



Government Integration enables verified communication between Guava and public institutions responsible for land administration, planning, taxation, infrastructure, and property regulation.



Rather than replacing government systems, Guava acts as an intelligent interoperability platform that synchronizes trusted property information where integrations are available.



Every government integration is modular, auditable, and permission-controlled.



\---



\# Vision



Governments become trusted participants within the Guava ecosystem.



Through standardized interfaces, authorized agencies can:



\- Verify records

\- Publish updates

\- Receive notifications

\- Validate permits

\- Support planning

\- Improve transparency



Every interaction strengthens the quality of the Property Passport.

\# Workflow Statement



Government Integration connects Guava with public institutions through secure, transparent, and standards-based interfaces.



By enriching Property Passports with authoritative records, planning decisions, survey information, and regulatory updates, governments become trusted contributors to a global property intelligence ecosystem while maintaining full ownership and control of their official data.
