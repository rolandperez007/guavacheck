\# Property Registration Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for registering a property within the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



Property Registration is the entry point into the Guava platform.



Every property, regardless of country, ownership type, or construction stage, enters the ecosystem through this workflow.



Registration creates the foundation for every subsequent capability including:



\- Verification

\- Property Passport

\- Mortgage

\- Construction

\- Digital Twin

\- Investment Analysis

\- Government Integration

\- Austin Intelligence



A registered property becomes a living digital asset.



\---



\# Objectives



The workflow should:



Reduce registration time



Maximize data quality



Minimize manual typing



Prevent duplicate records



Prepare properties for verification



Support every country



Allow progressive completion
