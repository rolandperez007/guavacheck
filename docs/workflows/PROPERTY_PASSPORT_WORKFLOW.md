\# Property Passport Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for creating, maintaining, and evolving a Property Passport throughout the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



The Property Passport is the permanent digital identity of a property.



It is not a document.



It is a continuously evolving intelligence record that aggregates trusted information from every participating system.



A Property Passport grows throughout the lifetime of a property.



As new information becomes available, the passport becomes increasingly valuable.



Every property should have one canonical passport.



\---

Create a permanent digital identity
