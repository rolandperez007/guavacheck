\# World Engine Workflow



> The official spatial intelligence workflow for Austin OS and the ACOS Runtime.



\---



\# Overview



The World Engine provides Austin with a continuously evolving understanding of the physical world.



Rather than viewing properties as isolated assets, Austin understands every property within its surrounding geographic, economic, environmental, social, legal, and infrastructure context.



The World Engine becomes Austin's spatial intelligence system.
