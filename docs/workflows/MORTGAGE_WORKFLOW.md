\# Mortgage Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for mortgage origination, comparison, approval, and lifecycle management within the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



The Mortgage Workflow connects verified properties, qualified applicants, Austin Intelligence, and participating financial institutions into a unified financing platform.



Unlike conventional mortgage systems, Guava begins with trusted property intelligence rather than disconnected paperwork.



Austin continuously assists borrowers, banks, developers, investors, and mortgage advisors throughout the financing lifecycle.



\---



\# Objectives



The workflow should:



Reduce approval time



Improve lending confidence



Minimize documentation duplication
