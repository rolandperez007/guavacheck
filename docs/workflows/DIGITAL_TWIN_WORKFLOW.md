\# Digital Twin Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for creating, synchronizing, simulating, and maintaining Digital Twins throughout the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



A Digital Twin is the living virtual representation of a real property.



Unlike static 3D models, the Digital Twin continuously evolves as the physical property changes.



It synchronizes verified information from:



Property Passport



Construction



Austin Intelligence



World Engine



Government Records



Maps



AI Vision



Future IoT integrations



Every twin represents the current understanding of a property.



\---



\# Vision



Every verified property should eventually have a continuously evolving Digital Twin.



The Digital Twin becomes:



The visual identity



The engineering record



The construction record



The maintenance record



The simulation environment



The renovation workspace



The planning environment



The operational intelligence layer
