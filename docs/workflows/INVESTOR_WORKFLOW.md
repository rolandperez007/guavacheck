\# Investor Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for discovering, evaluating, financing, acquiring, monitoring, and optimizing property investments within the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



The Investor Workflow transforms Guava into a comprehensive investment operating environment.



Rather than presenting isolated property listings, the platform enables investors to make informed decisions using verified property intelligence, Austin-powered analysis, market data, construction insights, financing options, and long-term portfolio monitoring.



Every investment opportunity is evaluated through trusted data and transparent reasoning.



\---



\# Objectives



The workflow should:



Identify high-quality investment opportunities



Reduce investment risk



Provide explainable analytics



Support institutional and retail investors



Enable portfolio management



Integrate financing and construction data



Continuously monitor investment performance
