\# Construction Workflow



> The official end-to-end specification for planning, estimating, executing, monitoring, and completing construction projects within the Guava ecosystem.



\---



\# Overview



The Construction Workflow transforms a verified property into an active construction project.



Construction is not treated as an isolated module.



It is deeply connected to:



\- Property Passport

\- Austin Intelligence

\- Digital Twin

\- World Engine

\- Mortgage Engine

\- Investor Engine

\- Government Systems

\- Contractor Network



Every construction project becomes a continuously updated digital asset.



\---



\# Objectives



The workflow should:



Reduce planning errors

Monitor project progress



Improve transparency
