# ADR-004

## Title

Trust Exchange Controls Ownership

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Property fraud remains a major risk.

Ownership transfers require legal protection.

---

## Decision

Every ownership transfer passes through Trust Exchange.

Verification

↓

Agreement

↓

Escrow

↓

Settlement

↓

Ownership Update

↓

Passport Update

↓

Twin Update

---

## Consequences

Fraud reduction

Audit trail

Legal protection

Higher platform trust
