\# OpenAI Attribution



\## Purpose



GuavaCheck uses OpenAI technology for selected artificial intelligence

capabilities.



Where appropriate, generated content may carry the attribution:



> AI generated with OpenAI



The attribution identifies the technology provider without suggesting that

OpenAI owns, operates, sponsors, endorses, or certifies GuavaCheck.



\## Product Identity



GuavaCheck and Austin remain the primary product identities.



OpenAI attribution must not:



\- replace GuavaCheck branding;

\- replace Austin branding;

\- imply an OpenAI partnership that has not been formally established;

\- imply endorsement or sponsorship by OpenAI;

\- modify or recreate OpenAI logos;

\- use OpenAI branding as part of GuavaCheck's own trademark or identity.



\## Attribution Layers



The platform may expose OpenAI attribution through:



1\. AI-generated user-facing content.

2\. AI operation metadata.

3\. AI audit/provenance records.

4\. Product documentation.



\## Current Attribution



Provider:



OpenAI



User-facing attribution:



AI generated with OpenAI



Disclosure:



This content was generated with the assistance of OpenAI technology.



\## Logo Policy



The initial implementation uses text attribution rather than embedding an

OpenAI logo.



If an OpenAI logo is introduced later, it must use the official asset and

follow the applicable OpenAI brand guidelines.



\## Architecture



OpenAI attribution belongs to the AI/provider layer.



It is independent of:



\- authentication;

\- identity;

\- users;

\- authorization;

\- billing;

\- property data;

\- Austin identity.



This separation allows GuavaCheck to support multiple AI providers while

accurately identifying the provider used for an individual operation.
