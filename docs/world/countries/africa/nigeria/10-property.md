# Property Overview

Nigeria operates a mixed land ownership framework governed primarily by the Land Use Act (1978).

Property ownership generally falls into:

- Residential
- Commercial
- Industrial
- Agricultural
- Institutional
- Hospitality
- Mixed-use
- Special Purpose

Austin must understand every category independently.

# Land Ownership

The Land Use Act places all land under the control of State Governors.

Occupancy rights are granted through statutory and customary mechanisms.

Austin should distinguish between:

- Statutory Right of Occupancy
- Customary Right of Occupancy
