# National Housing Fund (NHF)

Purpose

National savings programme supporting affordable home ownership.

Eligible Participants

- Salaried Employees
- Self-employed (subject to scheme requirements)
- Contributors meeting statutory requirements

Benefits

- Lower Interest Rates
- Longer Repayment Periods
- Government-backed Housing Finance
