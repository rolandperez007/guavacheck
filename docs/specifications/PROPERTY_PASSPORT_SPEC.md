# Property Passport Specification

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

## Purpose

The Property Passport is the permanent digital identity of every property on the Guava Platform.

Each property owns exactly one Property Passport.

The Property Passport remains with the property throughout its lifetime regardless of ownership changes.

---

## Objectives

Provide trusted property identity.

Track complete ownership history.

Store verified legal documentation.

Connect every platform engine.

Support Digital Twins.

Support Property DNA.

Support financing.

Support compliance.
