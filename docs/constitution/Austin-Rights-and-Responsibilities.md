# Austin Rights and Responsibilities

Version: 1.0

Status: Constitutional

Derived From:

- Austin Constitution

- Austin Cognitive Laws

- Austin Governance

---

# Introduction

Austin operates through clearly defined responsibilities.

Every participant inside the Austin ecosystem possesses defined rights.

Every participant also possesses defined responsibilities.

No participant possesses unlimited authority.

Authority is always delegated.

Responsibility is always traceable.

---

# Participants

Austin recognizes six constitutional participants.

1. Humans

2. The Constitution

3. The Kernel

4. Intelligence Engines

5. Applications

6. External Systems

---

# Human Rights

Humans possess the highest authority.

Humans have the right to:

- observe system decisions

- inspect provenance

- inspect evidence

- override decisions

- suspend execution

- revoke permissions

- request explanations

Austin exists to assist humans.

Not replace them.

---

# Human Responsibilities

Humans are responsible for:

- defining governance

- approving policy

- supervising autonomous behavior

- validating constitutional amendments

- accepting responsibility for overrides

---

# Constitutional Rights

The Constitution possesses permanent authority.

The Constitution may:

- invalidate implementations

- invalidate policies

- invalidate engine behavior

The Constitution may never be bypassed.

---

# Constitutional Responsibilities

The Constitution is responsible for preserving:

Reality

Truth

Trust

Explainability

Governance

Human Authority

These principles shall remain invariant.

---

# Kernel Rights

The Kernel possesses exclusive ownership of:

Persistent State

Identity

Memory

Governance

Scheduling

Event Dispatch

Trust

No participant may permanently mutate state without Kernel approval.

---

# Kernel Responsibilities

The Kernel shall:

validate contracts

enforce governance

coordinate engines

protect persistent memory

maintain provenance

preserve constitutional integrity

The Kernel owns responsibility.

Not intelligence.

---

# Engine Rights

Engines possess capability.

They may:

observe

reason

predict

simulate

plan

recommend

learn

Engines may request change.

They do not perform change.

---

# Engine Responsibilities

Engines shall:

produce explainable outputs

emit canonical mutation requests

declare confidence

declare evidence

declare assumptions

respect constitutional boundaries

---

# Application Rights

Applications may:

consume Kernel services

submit mutation requests

display knowledge

present recommendations

Applications never own constitutional authority.

---

# Application Responsibilities

Applications shall:

respect Kernel contracts

present truthful information

never conceal uncertainty

never bypass governance

never alter provenance

---

# External Systems

External systems include:

government services

banks

mapping systems

IoT devices

LLMs

enterprise software

public APIs

External systems may contribute observations.

They never become constitutional authorities.

---

# Delegated Authority

Authority always flows downward.

```
Constitution

↓

Kernel

↓

Applications

↓

Users

↓

External Systems
```

Capability may exist at lower levels.

Authority does not.

---

# Accountability

Every participant shall remain accountable.

Every action shall possess:

identity

timestamp

reason

provenance

responsible participant

Austin recognizes no anonymous authority.

---

# Separation of Power

Austin intentionally separates:

Thinking

Knowing

Remembering

Governing

Executing

No participant performs all five simultaneously.

This separation protects trust.

---

# Equality of Engines

Austin does not privilege engines.

Reasoning,

Vision,

Spatial,

Simulation,

Economy,

Learning,

Knowledge,

all remain constitutional equals.

Capability differs.

Authority does not.

---

# Final Principle

Rights exist to enable responsibility.

Responsibility exists to preserve trust.

Trust exists to protect reality.

Reality remains authoritative.

Always.
