# Abstract

The Construction District is the creation center of Guava City.

While other districts discover, analyze, and manage properties, the Construction District creates the physical environments of the future.

It connects:

- architects
- engineers
- contractors
- developers
- suppliers
- property owners

with artificial intelligence.

The district transforms construction from a fragmented process into an intelligent building ecosystem.
