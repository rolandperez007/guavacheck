# Abstract

The Community District is the human connection layer of Guava City.

While other districts focus on properties, intelligence, finance, and construction, the Community District focuses on the people who create meaning around places.

A city is not only buildings.

A city is people.

The Community District connects:

- residents
- professionals
- property owners
- developers
- experts
- local communities

into one intelligent network.
