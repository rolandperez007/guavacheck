# Abstract

The Digital Twin District is the immersive reality layer of Guava City.

It creates intelligent digital representations of:

- properties
- buildings
- communities
- construction projects
- environments

The district connects physical reality with digital intelligence.

Users can explore, modify, simulate, and understand spaces before making decisions.
