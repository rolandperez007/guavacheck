# Abstract

The Commerce Services District is the economic support layer of Guava City.

While the Commerce District connects people with properties, the Commerce Services District connects properties with everything required to create, improve, maintain, and operate them.

A property is not complete when it is purchased.

A property becomes valuable through:

- design
- maintenance
- improvements
- services
- human expertise

This district creates the service economy surrounding every property.
