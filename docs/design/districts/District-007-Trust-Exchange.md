# Abstract

The Trust Exchange is the foundation of confidence inside Guava City.

It exists to answer the most important question in property:

> Can this information be trusted?

The Trust Exchange transforms verification from a hidden process into a visible intelligence layer.

It protects:

- buyers
- sellers
- investors
- institutions
- communities

by connecting every important object to evidence and provenance.
