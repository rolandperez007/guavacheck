\# Abstract



The Residential District UI represents the living heart of Guava City.



The interface transforms property search into a lifestyle discovery experience.



Users should not feel they are searching through houses.



They should feel they are discovering a future home.



The Residential District communicates:



\- comfort

\- belonging

\- lifestyle

\- safety

\- aspiration
