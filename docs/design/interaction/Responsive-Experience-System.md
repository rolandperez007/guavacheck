# Abstract

The Guava City Responsive Experience System defines how the digital city adapts across devices, screen sizes, interaction methods, and environments.

Guava City is not simply resized between devices.

It transforms.

The experience changes according to the user's context while preserving:

- identity
- navigation
- intelligence
- trust
- continuity

---

# 1. Responsive Philosophy

The core principle:

> The city changes shape, not identity.

A user should recognize Guava City whether they are using:

- desktop
- tablet
- mobile
- wearable devices
- future spatial environments

# 4. Tablet Experience

Tablet represents exploration mode.

Primary users:

- property seekers
- agents
- field professionals

---

## Tablet Characteristics

Provides:

- simplified districts
- touch navigation
- focused workflows
- property exploration

---

## Tablet Philosophy

Less information density.

More interaction.

The city becomes easier to explore physically.

---

# 5. Mobile Experience

The mobile experience is not a smaller desktop.

It is an Austin companion.

4. Important information
