# Property System Overview

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Living Document

**Classification:** Internal Platform Documentation

---

# Purpose

The Property System is the authoritative source of truth for every property represented within guavacheck.

It manages the complete lifecycle of property intelligence, from discovery and verification through valuation, investment analysis and long-term historical tracking.

The Property System enables Austin to reason about real estate using structured, trustworthy and continuously evolving information.

---

# Vision

The Property System will become one of the world's largest structured property intelligence platforms.

Rather than storing simple property listings, guavacheck will maintain complete digital identities for properties.

Every property becomes a living asset with history, relationships, intelligence and context.

---

# Core Objectives

The system shall:

• Organize property information

• Verify authenticity

• Support intelligent search

• Analyze investment potential

• Track market changes

• Preserve historical records

• Assist enterprise management

• Enable government planning
