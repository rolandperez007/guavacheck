# Vision Engine

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Vision Engine enables Austin to interpret and reason about visual information.

Rather than processing only text, Austin will understand images, drawings, maps, floor plans and 3D models.

---

# Responsibilities

The Vision Engine shall:

- Analyze property images.
- Interpret architectural drawings.
- Understand floor plans.
- Review site photographs.
- Detect structural features.
- Compare design alternatives.
- Assist renovation planning.

---

# Supported Inputs

- Images
- Floor plans
- Drone photography
- Satellite imagery
- Site surveys
- CAD exports
- BIM models
- 3D renders

---

# Example Use Cases

- Estimate construction progress.
- Compare planned versus actual layouts.
- Detect design inconsistencies.
- Suggest renovation opportunities.
- Analyze land suitability.

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may include:

- Live video understanding
- Construction site monitoring
- Structural defect detection
- AI-assisted design feedback
- Digital twin visualization
