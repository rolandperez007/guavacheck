# Austin Decision Flow

Version: 1.0.0

---

# Overview

Every Austin interaction follows a structured decision pipeline.

The objective is to produce reliable, explainable and consistent outcomes.

---

# Decision Pipeline

```
Receive Request

↓

Understand Intent

↓

Retrieve Context

↓

Consult Memory

↓

Consult Knowledge

↓

Reason

↓

Create Plan

↓

Select Platform Systems

↓

Execute Actions

↓

Validate Results

↓

Generate Response

↓

Learn
```

---

# Decision Stages

## 1. Receive Request

Accept input from user or platform.

---

## 2. Understand Intent

Identify user objectives.

---

## 3. Retrieve Context

Load previous conversations.

Load project state.

Load user preferences.

---

## 4. Consult Knowledge

Identify relevant knowledge.

Retrieve applicable rules.

---

## 5. Reason

Evaluate available options.

Resolve ambiguity.

Identify risks.

---

## 6. Plan

Determine required actions.

Sequence execution.

---

## 7. Execute

Coordinate platform systems.

Collect outputs.

---

## 8. Validate

Verify consistency.

Detect conflicts.

Ensure confidence.

---

## 9. Respond

Present clear recommendations.

Provide explanations when appropriate.

---

## 10. Learn

Capture anonymous platform improvements.

Update internal learning models where applicable.

---

# Design Goal

Every Austin decision should be:

Consistent.

Explainable.

Helpful.

Reliable.
