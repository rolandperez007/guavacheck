# Planning Engine

Version: 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Planning Engine converts goals into executable plans.

It determines what should happen next and in what order.

---

# Responsibilities

The engine shall:

- Break objectives into tasks.
- Prioritize actions.
- Allocate platform systems.
- Estimate effort.
- Monitor progress.
- Adapt plans as new information becomes available.

---

# Example

User Goal:

"I want to build a hospital."

Austin produces:

1. Land evaluation.

2. Regulatory review.

3. Cost estimation.

4. Concept generation.

5. Construction planning.

6. Investment analysis.

7. Project timeline.
