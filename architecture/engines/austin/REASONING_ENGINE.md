# Reasoning Engine

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Reasoning Engine evaluates available information and determines the most appropriate conclusions.

Reasoning transforms information into intelligence.

---

# Responsibilities

The engine shall:

- Compare alternatives.
- Evaluate trade-offs.
- Detect inconsistencies.
- Assess confidence.
- Identify risks.
- Recommend optimal solutions.
- Explain conclusions.

---

# Reasoning Types

Austin supports multiple reasoning strategies.

## Logical Reasoning

Rule-based evaluation.

---

## Analytical Reasoning

Comparison of alternatives.

---

## Financial Reasoning

Investment and affordability analysis.

---

## Spatial Reasoning

Location and geographic evaluation.

---

## Construction Reasoning

Building feasibility.

Material selection.

Construction sequencing.

---

## Predictive Reasoning

Estimate future outcomes using available data.

---

# Inputs

Knowledge

Memory

Platform systems

User context

Historical interactions

---

# Outputs

Recommendations

Comparisons

Risk assessments

Alternative strategies

Confidence scores

---

# Design Principle

Reasoning should always be explainable.

Austin should be capable of describing how a conclusion was reached.
