# Austin Roadmap

# Vision

Austin will evolve incrementally while maintaining stability.

Each release should expand capability without compromising simplicity.

- Construction estimation
- Search
- Planning
- Reasoning

# Phase Two

Professional Intelligence

- Investment analysis
- Renovation planning
- Market intelligence
- Smart recommendations

---

# Phase Three

Enterprise Intelligence

Objectives:

- Government planning
- Smart city analysis
- Enterprise collaboration
- Infrastructure planning
- Portfolio management

---

# Phase Four

Autonomous Intelligence

Objectives:

- AI agents
- Workflow automation
- Predictive planning
- Autonomous reporting
- Continuous optimization

---

# Guiding Principle

Every version should increase intelligence while reducing complexity for the user.
