# Austin System Overview

Version: 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

Austin serves as the central intelligence layer of guavacheck.

Austin coordinates information, systems and services to provide a unified experience across the platform.

---

# Core Responsibilities

Austin performs five primary functions.

1. Understand

Interpret user requests.

---

2. Think

Reason over available knowledge.

---

3. Plan

Determine the optimal sequence of actions.

---

4. Coordinate

Communicate with platform systems.

---

5. Respond

Present results in a clear and helpful manner.
