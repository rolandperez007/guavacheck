# ADR-0003: Knowledge Graph as the Intelligence Foundation

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-17

---

# Context

Austin must reason across relationships rather than isolated records.

Properties relate to communities.

Communities relate to cities.

Cities relate to infrastructure.

Infrastructure affects investment.

Traditional relational models alone do not sufficiently represent these interconnected relationships.

---

# Decision

Austin shall reason over a platform-wide Knowledge Graph.

Business systems contribute knowledge.

Austin consumes relationships.

The Knowledge Graph becomes the semantic layer connecting every platform domain.

# Consequences

Positive

- Better reasoning.
- Richer recommendations.
- Explainable intelligence.
- Cross-domain analysis.
- Future digital twins.

Trade-offs

- Greater architectural complexity.
- Graph maintenance.
- Relationship versioning.

---

# Alternatives Considered

Independent databases.

Rejected.

Reason:

Relationships become fragmented.

---

# Guiding Principle

Knowledge is defined not only by entities but also by the relationships connecting them.
