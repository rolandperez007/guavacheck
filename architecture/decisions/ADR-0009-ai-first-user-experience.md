# ADR-0009: AI-First User Experience

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-17

---

# Context

Many platforms present AI as an additional feature.

guavacheck intends for intelligence to become the primary method through which users interact with the platform.

---

# Decision

Austin shall serve as the primary intelligence interface.

Traditional navigation, search and dashboards remain available, but AI becomes the preferred interaction model.

Austin should assist users in:

- Discovery.
- Planning.
- Decision making.
- Analysis.
- Recommendations.
- Automation.

The user should experience intelligence rather than complexity.

---

# Consequences

Positive

- More natural interactions.
- Reduced learning curve.
- Consistent platform experience.

Trade-offs

- Greater emphasis on explainability.
- Higher expectations for response quality.

---

# Alternatives Considered

Traditional application with optional AI.

Rejected.

Reason:

Limits the transformational potential of the platform.

---

# Guiding Principle

AI should quietly make the impossible feel effortless.
