# Engineering Principles

These principles guide every engineering decision made within guavacheck.

---

## 1. Stack on Foundations

Every feature must strengthen the platform.

Never sacrifice architecture for speed.

---

## 2. Intelligence Before Complexity

Technology exists to simplify.

Never overwhelm users.

---

## 3. Premium by Default

Every interaction should feel intentional.

---

## 4. Austin Orchestrates

Austin coordinates engines.

Austin does not become the engine.

---

## 5. Every Engine Has One Responsibility

Small responsibilities.

Clear boundaries.

Simple maintenance.

---

## 6. Build for Scale

Design systems that can evolve from hundreds of users to millions.

Scaling should be architectural rather than reactive.

---

## 7. Global by Design

Localization is not an afterthought.

Every user deserves a native experience.

---

## 8. Trust is a Product Feature

Verification.

Privacy.

Security.

Transparency.

These are core capabilities.

---

## 9. Continuous Evolution

Software is never finished.

Every release should improve the platform.

---

## 10. Simplicity Wins

Elegant systems outperform complicated ones.

Remove complexity whenever possible.

---

## Platform Motto

AI quietly making the impossible feel effortless.

---

## Engineering Motto

Build for decades.

Not deployments.
