# guavacheck

**The Global AI Operating System for the Built World**

Developed by **Guava Networks Inc.**

---

## Vision

guavacheck is an AI-powered platform that assists homeowners, developers, contractors, architects, engineers, investors, insurers, and governments throughout the complete building lifecycle.

Rather than serving a single country or region, guavacheck is designed as a **global infrastructure platform** capable of adapting to local regulations, currencies, payment systems, construction practices, languages, and purchasing power.

Our mission is simple:

> Every person on Earth deserves intelligent access to planning, building, buying, protecting, and managing property.

---

## Core Components

### Austin AI

Austin is the primary AI engine responsible for:

* Construction intelligence
* Cost estimation
* Project planning
* Building guidance
* Property valuation
* Risk analysis
* Global knowledge reasoning

---

### GWIE (Global World Intelligence Engine)

GWIE enables guavacheck to operate internationally.

Responsibilities include:

* Country intelligence
* Local currencies
* Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)
* Time zones
* Languages
* Construction standards
* Regional regulations
* Payment routing
* Localization

---

### IronGate

Enterprise-grade security layer.

Provides:

* Authentication
* Authorization
* API validation
* Fraud detection
* Reputation scoring
* Risk analysis

---

### Distress Marketplace

AI-assisted marketplace for distressed assets.

Supports:

* Sellers
* Buyers
* Agents
* Banks
* Receivers
* Legal workflows

---

### Building Passport

Digital identity for every property.

Stores:

* Plans
* Materials
* Maintenance
* Ownership history
* Certifications
* Compliance records
