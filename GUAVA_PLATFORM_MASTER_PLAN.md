# Executive Summary

Guava is an AI-powered Property Intelligence Platform that unifies Property Identity, Digital Twins, Construction, Finance, Commerce, Investment, Trust and Knowledge into a single connected ecosystem.

Unlike traditional listing websites, Guava manages the complete lifecycle of a property—from creation to ownership, financing, maintenance, investment and resale.

Every property is represented by a unique Property Passport and can be visualised through Twin Studio (3D), analysed by Austin AI and connected to a global ecosystem of trusted partners.
